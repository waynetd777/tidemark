    Kept on your Mac.

    Nothing leaves.

    Your history stays in Tidemark's folder on your Mac, and nothing leaves it unless you use Ask. No Tidemark account or server, no tracking.

    Light on its feet.

    Written in Rust. It reads every few seconds and slows down when you're not looking.

    Never averaged away.

    Each minute keeps its average and its peak, so a short spike still shows.

    Sleep isn't a dip.

    Time asleep or not recording is shaded, never drawn as zero.

    Help on every screen.

    Free, and open.

    Questions.

    How is it different from Activity Monitor?

    Activity Monitor shows this moment. Tidemark records every minute for up to 14 days, and every day for a year, so you can go back to Wednesday afternoon and see what was happening, or to last spring. It also maps the disk and marks the week's highs.

    Does it slow my Mac down?

    It reads every 2 seconds while you're watching and every 5 when you're not, and redraws at most once a minute when its window is behind others. Disk scans run once a day by default, when the Mac has been idle for 5 minutes and is on power (or after two days, if it never is), and when you open it if the last scan is over an hour old. You can choose hourly, which costs some battery, or off, which scans only when you press Reload.

    Why does it want Full Disk Access?

    To see inside Mail, Safari, Photos and other protected folders on the Disk Map. Without it those folders are skipped and counted, and a banner says so. Everything else works without it.

    Where is my history kept?

    In Tidemark's folder in your Library, on this Mac only: an SQLite file for the history, beside the saved scans and your settings. Choose 3, 7 or 14 days of every minute in Settings; a row a day of every measure, and of disk space, is kept for a year.

    Does Ask send my data anywhere?

    Only when you ask, and only to the AI tool you chose, signed in with your own account: Claude Code sends it to Anthropic, Codex to OpenAI, Antigravity to Google and Copilot to GitHub. It is given names, paths, sizes and figures, and a crash report's summary when you ask about one. Tidemark works fully without it.

    Is it free? Does it run on Intel or Windows?

    It's free and open source under the GNU GPL. For now it's for Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later.

    Know your Mac.

    Install it once and it keeps watch from the menu bar.

    Download for Mac View on GitHub ›

    Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 13 or later.

    1. Open the disk image and drag Tidemark onto Applications.
    2. Open it once. macOS says it can't check the app, because it isn't notarised by Apple. Click Done.
    3. In System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, then Open Anyway again when macOS asks. It opens normally after that.
    4. For the whole Disk Map, turn Tidemark on under Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access, then press Reload.
    5. To keep recording while it's quit, turn on Keep recording when quit in Settings. It appears as Tidemark under General › Login Items › Allow in the Background.
