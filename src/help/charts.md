## Reading the charts

Every chart reads the same way, and its legend names each mark.

| Mark | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Line** | The average over each point: a minute on the 1-hour range, a few minutes on longer ones (the legend says how many). It ends at the live reading. |
| **Band** | On Performance, the busiest moment within each point, so a spike of a few seconds still shows. |
| **Dashed line** | The **tidemark**, where a chart has one: the week's high (on Trends, the range's). A dot and label mark the range's peak. |
| **Dotted line** | **Now**, at the right-hand end. |
| **Soft band** | **Asleep**: nothing runs while the Mac sleeps, so sleep is a gap, not a dip to zero. |
| **Hatched band** | **Not recording**: Tidemark was quit, paused or not yet started. |

- Point at a chart to read any moment, or at a gap for its times.
- **Colour** marks trouble. When the headline is about a measure, its number and line turn amber (a warning) or red (critical), with a word saying which. Everything else stays in the tide colour.
