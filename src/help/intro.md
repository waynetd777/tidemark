# Tidemark

Tidemark records what is using your Mac's disk and how hard the Mac is working, now and over time, and marks the highs: the tidemarks.

- Four screens in the sidebar: **Now** (⌘1), **Recent** (⌘2), **Trends** (⌘3) and **Disk Map** (⌘4), with **Settings** (⌘,) below. Tidemark opens on the screen you last had open.
- Now is the moment and the last hour, Recent every minute of the last hour to 14 days, Trends every day of the last month to a year.
- Below the screens the sidebar shows **This Mac**, and on Disk Map the scan: the folder, the disk's space and the last scan.
- **‹ ›** (⌘[ and ⌘]) step back and forward through screens and folders, like Finder.
- This guide is the app's help. Press **?** (or Help › Tidemark Help) for the sections about the screen you are on; **The whole guide** shows them all, and typing searches every section.
- Help › Tidemark Website opens tidemark.davies.co.za.
