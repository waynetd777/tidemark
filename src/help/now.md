## Now

How your computer is doing, at a glance.

- A sentence at the top says how the computer is doing: about the worst of what needs attention, if anything does. Every measure that needs attention is coloured amber or red on its card.
- Eight cards in rows of four: CPU, GPU (where the computer reports it), memory, disk I/O, network download, network upload and energy over the last hour, and disk space over the last 24 hours. Each has a meter of how full it is now.
- The CPU card's chevron shows each core, performance cores and efficiency cores apart where the processor has both kinds<!-- windows --> (Intel's 12th generation and later)<!-- /windows -->; the choice is remembered.<!-- mac -->
- **Using the most memory**, **CPU**, **disk I/O** and **energy right now**, two by two: the five apps using the most of each, live, with another measure beside each name. An app's helper processes count as the app.<!-- /mac --><!-- github --> (Mac)<!-- /github --><!-- windows -->
- **Using the most memory**, **CPU** and **disk I/O right now**: the five apps using the most of each, live, with another measure beside each name. An app's helper processes count as the app, and programs run as administrator, other sessions' and protected ones such as Microsoft Defender count too, as in Task Manager. Windows doesn't measure apps' energy, so there is no list for it.<!-- /windows -->
- Below, three lists side by side: **This week's tidemarks** (the week's peaks and incidents), **Crashes and hangs this week**, and **What grew on disk this week**, where the disk's growth landed.
- **Details** on a card opens Recent at that measure's tab.
- The questions at the foot open Ask with the question typed in.
