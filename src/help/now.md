## Now

How your Mac is doing, at a glance.

- A sentence at the top says how the Mac is doing.
- Eight cards in rows of four: CPU, GPU (where the Mac reports it), memory, disk I/O, network download, network upload and energy over the last hour, and disk space over the last 24 hours.
- **This week's tidemarks** lists the week's peaks, incidents and apps that crashed or hung.
- **What grew on disk this week** lists the folders where the disk's growth landed.
- **Details** on a card opens Performance and scrolls to that measure.
- The questions at the foot open Ask with the question typed in.
