## Now

How your Mac is doing, at a glance.

- A sentence at the top says how the Mac is doing: about the worst of what needs attention, if anything does. Every measure that needs attention is coloured amber or red on its card.
- Eight cards in rows of four: CPU, GPU (where the Mac reports it), memory, disk I/O, network download, network upload and energy over the last hour, and disk space over the last 24 hours. Each has a meter of how full it is now.
- The CPU card's chevron shows each core, performance cores and efficiency cores apart; the choice is remembered.
- Below, three lists side by side: **This week's tidemarks** (the week's peaks and incidents), **Crashes and hangs this week**, and **What grew on disk this week**, where the disk's growth landed.
- **Details** on a card opens Recent at that measure's tab.
- The questions at the foot open Ask with the question typed in.
