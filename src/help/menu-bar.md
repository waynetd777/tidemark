## Menu bar

Tidemark keeps recording in the menu bar after you close its window.

- Closing the window also takes Tidemark out of the Dock.
- The icon gets a mark when something needs attention: memory running high, the CPU busy, swap growing, a low battery, a nearly full disk.
- Click it for a small window: a sentence on how the Mac is doing, and CPU, memory, network download, energy and disk space, each with a line for the last hour (the disk's for the last 24 hours, as on Now) and today's high.
- Click a measure to open Recent at its card.
- Below them: what is using the most right now, and a line about the week when there is something to say (an app holding much of your memory, or the disk growing by a gigabyte or more).
- When something needs attention, the main action takes you to it (Show what's using memory, say).
- Then **Open Tidemark** (⌘O), **Settings** (⌘,) and **Quit** (⌘Q). ⌘2 and ⌘4 open Recent and Disk Map; Esc closes the window.
- Quitting stops recording, unless the background helper is on.
