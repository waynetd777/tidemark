## Trends

The disk's space over weeks and months, and when the Mac is busiest.

- Ranges: 7 days, 14 days, 30 days, 3 months or 12 months. The one you pick is remembered.
- **Disk space used**: how much it grew, how fast, and when the disk would be full at that rate. The line is hourly up to 14 days, each day's high beyond that.
- The tidemark is the most it held in the range; the list beside it names the biggest one-day rises.
- **Top folders that rose**: where growth landed. Up to 14 days it compares scans; beyond, the daily record of the 40 biggest folders, which is coarser.
- **When it gets busy**: each weekday hour shaded by its average CPU, darker for busier. Point at a cell for its value.
- At the foot, the range's highs in Now's order: CPU, GPU, memory, disk I/O, download, upload, energy and disk space. The first seven cover up to 14 days (the history kept); disk space covers the whole range.
