## Trends

Every measure over a month to a year, a day at a time.

- Ranges: 30 days, 3 months or 12 months. Recent covers the last hour to 14 days. The range and tab you pick are remembered.
- Tidemark keeps a row for each day for a year: each measure's average and peak, what was read, written, downloaded and uploaded, the energy used, the day's incidents and crashes, and its busiest apps. Trends fills in a day at a time, and says how far back it goes until it covers the range.
- **All** shows one chart a measure. The other tabs give one measure more room: **CPU** with its performance and efficiency cores, **GPU** beside the CPU and power, **Memory** with the swap and pressure highs, **Disk I/O** and **Network** as each day's totals, **Energy** with the power drawn and what draws it, and **Disk space**.
- Each chart's line is the day's figure (its average, or its total) and the band the day's peak; Disk space's line is each day's high, with no band. Click a day, or its peak dot, to see it in the card on the right: the day's figures and its busiest apps, averaged over the minutes each ran. Until you click, it shows the range's busiest day.
- **Records**: the busiest day, the highest peak and the busiest week of the range. Click one to see that day.
- **When it gets busy**: each weekday hour shaded by the tab's measure, from the last 14 days (or as many as are kept). Point at a cell for its value.
- **Disk space**: how much it grew, how fast, and when the disk would be full at that rate, with the range's high and its biggest one-day rises. **Top folders that rose** compares the daily record of the 40 biggest folders.
